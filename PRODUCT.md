# HeadPain

## Register

product

## Users

People describing head and neck pain to a clinician, a partner, or someone at work. They may be using a phone while in pain, with limited attention and tolerance for bright screens. Readers of a shared map need to understand it without learning the editor.

## Product Purpose

Turn the location, intensity, depth, quality, and everyday impact of pain into a clear visual description. Support a private episode diary and portable maps without accounts. HeadPain is a communication aid, not a diagnostic tool.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, and practical. Use the person's own language and explain each action at the moment it becomes useful.

## Anti-references

The repository explicitly excludes decorative seasonal effects from this clinical tool. Avoid colour-only pain indicators, unexplained controls, and maps whose legends disappear when shared. Avoid diagnosis claims or treating pattern similarity as a diagnosis.

## Design Principles

- Put the map and its next useful action within reach.
- Let the first point create its pain automatically.
- Give every pain both a colour and a shape, including in exported maps.
- Show the person's description in readable sentences.
- Keep the diary local and make sharing deliberate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve the existing colour-and-shape visual grammar for colour-blind readers and print. Provide named areas as an alternative to interacting with the 3D head. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, touch input, legible contrast, and reduced motion.

These product constraints come from README.md, CLAUDE.md, and the existing interface.
