Put a pain map inside your own page

The embed is the same 3D head and the same legend as the app, with the editor removed. Use it to show a reader what a migraine looks like next to a sinus infection, to let them turn the skin translucent and see how deep a pain sits, or to publish a map somebody made of their own pain. Configure it below and copy the snippet. Nothing is uploaded: everything the embed shows travels in the URL you paste.

What should it show?

Copy it from the app: Episodes → Copy link to show someone. The map itself is inside the link, so the embed needs no account and no server.

Driving it from your own page

The embed answers postMessage, so your page's own buttons can steer it: step a reader through a pattern paragraph by paragraph, or turn on x-ray exactly when the text starts talking about depth. Press these to drive the preview above. 



      

        The whole contract
        
URL settings

        

          
mapA share-link payload: the part after #m= in a HeadPain link.

          
presetA pattern from the library, for example preset=cluster-headache.

          
demoA demo map, for example demo=demo-combo.

          
xray1 starts with the skin translucent.

          
isolateShow one pain alone, by name or by number starting at 0.

          
legendcompact (default), full, or off.

          
controlsview (default) or none.

          
rotateauto turns the head until the visitor touches it. Ignored when the visitor's system asks for reduced motion.

          
spinSeconds per revolution when rotating. Default 24.

          
cameratheta,phi,distance, for example camera=2.4,1.4,4.2.

          
titleCaption text, or off to hide the caption bar.

          
bgSix-digit hex background, to blend with your page.

        

        
Messages you can send

        

          
headpain:xray{ on: true }

          
headpain:isolate{ pain: "Migraine" }, a number, or null for all

          
headpain:camera{ camera: [theta, phi, distance] }

          
headpain:resetback to the front view

          
headpain:stateask it to report what it is showing

        

        
Messages it sends back

        

          Every message carries source: "headpain". Types are ready,
          state, pain (the visitor picked one), zone (the
          visitor hovered an area of the head) and error.
        

        
What it will not do

        

          An embed never reads the visitor's saved diary. A HeadPain widget on your page can only
          show what you put in its URL, so it cannot expose a reader's own private maps.
